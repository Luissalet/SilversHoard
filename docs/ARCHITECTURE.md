# Silver's Hoard — architecture

## Pieces

```
browser (React UI, client/)  ──/api/ui/call──►  FastAPI app (silvers_hoard/)  ──JSON lines──►  Node engine (engine/src/server.mjs)
MCP client ──stdio── mcp_server.py ──/api/agent/call──►        │                                   @pkmn/sim + ai.mjs
                                                                ├── SQLite data/silver.db            (live battles in memory)
                                                                ├── jobs: node engine/src/cli.mjs  (series, tournaments, watched battles)
                                                                ├── sprites: Showdown sprite server → data/cache/sprites
                                                                ├── walking sprites (real time): PMDCollab SpriteCollab → data/cache/pmd
                                                                └── family: Hoard Hub (copilot, events, LLM, Prospero)
playable page (static/play/play.html) = React battle screen + engine/src (core, ai, match) inlined, data slot filled on export
real-time screen (client/src/battle/rt, lazy chunk) = engine/src/realtime run in the browser; the app replays its inputs with the same files
```

## Engine (`engine/src`, ES modules, also bundled into the playable page)

- `core.mjs` — formats (`buildFormatId` adds Showdown custom rules after `@@@`), team import/export (`@pkmn/sets`), `completeSets` (fills ability and Tera type), `applyLevelRules` (fixed level, cap, minimum: applied to the sets because Showdown only applies "Adjust Level" in its validator), validation (`TeamValidator`), random teams (`@pkmn/randoms`, seeded), Pokédex search and learnsets, team summaries.
- `points.mjs` — stat points as in Pokémon Champions (66 per Pokémon, 32 per stat): `evFromSp` / `spFromEv` (HOME's rule: 4 EVs the first point, 8 each more), `evsFromPoints` (limits checked), `pointsFromEvs`, `calcStat`. `teamSummary` adds `points`, `points_total` and `stats` at the set's level; engine command `stat_points`.
- `ai.mjs` — `chooseFor(battle, side, {level, style, rng})`. Reads a deep copy of the battle (`freeze` = `JSON.stringify(battle.toJSON())`, `Battle.fromJSON` of a fresh parse; `fromJSON` keeps references into its input, so a copy is never reused). Damage is `battle.actions.getDamage` on the copy (every generation's formula, abilities, items, weather), times accuracy and expected hits, with absorbing abilities handled. Status, setup, hazards, hazard removal, recovery, protection, screens, weather and terrain have their own scores, weighted by style. Switching weighs the best damage given against the best taken. Team preview orders by type matchup. Doubles: per slot with targets, spread moves, ally damage. Expert (singles): up to nine own options × the opponent's three best moves and two best switches, each played out on copies with two seeds, scored by an evaluation (HP, status, boosts, hazards, screens, KO), choosing 0.65 × weighted mean + 0.35 × worst case. Expert (doubles): each slot's three best moves (every target counts) and best switch, paired without sending one bench Pokémon twice or using two gimmicks, the twelve best pairings × the opponent's best pairing and the two with one slot on its second idea, same playouts and score, within 2.5 s. A playout chooses for the opponent first and then for the AI, so a choice a person already sent is replaced by the guess and never read. Revival Blessing's switch request is answered with a fainted Pokémon.
- `match.mjs` — `Match`: a battle with two seats; AI seats choose in `advance()`, a person's seat waits for `choose()`. `state(view, since)` returns the new lines for a seat (`viewLines` resolves `|split|` blocks), the pending requests annotated for screens (move type, power, category, description; team sprite ids, types, item and ability names) and the result. Turn limit → tie. `snapshot()` for resuming.
- `stats.mjs` — `summarize` (KOs, damage, faints per Pokémon from the log), `playOut`, `series`, `tournament` (bracket with byes, or round robin with points).
- `calc.mjs` — `damageCalc({attacker, defender, format, moves, attacker_boosts, defender_boosts, attacker_tera, defender_tera, attacker_mega, defender_mega, weather, terrain, screens, defender_hp})`: both Pokémon on a one-on-one battle of the format's generation (switch-in abilities and their weather happen, then the stated field, boosts, Tera and Mega Evolution win; a defender without moves gets Splash), each move prepared as it is used (`ModifyType` / `ModifyMove`: Pixilate, Weather Ball, Tera Blast; the AI's `estimate` does the same through `usedMove`), and `getDamage` with the randomizer pinned to each of the 16 rolls, with and without a critical hit: rolls, min/max (and % of max HP), crit range, rolls that knock out and hits to KO. Engine command `damage_calc`, tool `damage_calc` (agent_tools.py calls the engine directly), panel `client/src/components/DamageCalc.jsx` at the foot of the team builder.
- `server.mjs` — the line protocol (one JSON request per line, one answer per line, errors as data). `cli.mjs` — one-shot runs with progress on stderr.
- `scripts/build_canon.mjs` — parses pret's trainer data (Emerald and FireRed C sources, Crystal asm) into `silvers_hoard/presets/canon.json`: 79 trainers and 4 leagues; empty move slots get the last four level-up moves from Showdown's learnsets, like the games. `scripts/build_names_es.py` — Spanish names from PokeAPI's CSVs into `client/src/battle/names-es.js`. `scripts/build_characters.mjs` — turns `scripts/characters/bulbapedia_parties.json.gz` (Bulbapedia's `{{Party}}` / `{{Pokémon}}` templates of 155 character pages, read from each page's raw wikitext in a browser, since the site refuses scripted clients) into `silvers_hoard/presets/characters.json.gz`: 210 characters, 1,733 teams, with `bulbapedia_parties_2.json.gz` (Frontier Brains, villains' admins, more rivals) and `bulbapedia_facilities.json.gz` (`{{lop/facility}}` rows: battle facility teams with EVs and natures; pools of more than six keep `pool`). Each team has its game cards (`games`: a team of several games is in each) and a role (`category`: leaders, elite, champions, trials, rivals, villains, facilities, tournaments, others) from its class, its battle labels and the character. Every species, move, item, ability and nature goes through the dex of the team's generation (unknown ones are dropped and counted); levels with modes or ranges keep the normal one; tag battles keep the character's side; identical teams of one game merge their labels; rivals' teams per starter are told apart by the Pokémon only that team has; sentences about bag items (potions and the like) leave the note, since battles use held items only; each team gets the Showdown sprite of its game (`-gen1rb`, `-gen2`, `-lgpe`…); Spanish names and battle labels come from `scripts/characters/names_es.json` and `labels_es.json`.

## App (`silvers_hoard`)

- `engine.py` — starts and talks to the Node process (ids, timeouts, restart after a crash), and runs `cli.mjs` jobs with progress and cancel.
- `store.py` — SQLite (migrations V1-V3; V3 adds `badge_image` to gyms and earned badges): `trainers`, `gyms`, `leagues` (with trash), `battles` (seats with their teams, status live/running/ended/failed, omniscient log, summary, snapshot), `runs` (gym and league challenges and their stages), `badges`, `hall`, `jobs`.
- `services.py` — everything: trainers (create, update, random, duplicate, lines with the family's model), canon install (trainers, gyms with badges and level caps, leagues), characters (`characters_list`, `character_get`, `character_install`: a trainer from one team and one sprite, `canon_key` `char:<key>:<team>:<sprite>` so the same pick returns the same trainer, or an own team with `custom`), battles (play, hot-seat, watched; `since` counts lines of the live log; finishing stores the omniscient log, records wins and advances the challenge), challenges (stages, auto mode, badges, hall of fame, optional modern rules), series and tournaments (jobs; sample battles stored), sharing (files, codes, playable pages, result codes), avatars (pixel art, upload, Prospero job through Hoard Hub), settings.
- `sharing.py` — wrappers `silvers-hoard/<kind>`, codes `SILVER1-` / `SILVERWIN1-` (zlib + base64url), `parse_any` (codes, JSON files, playable pages, Showdown text), the playable page writer (fills the `silver-data` script tag).
- `sprites.py` — the sprite cache (download once, remember 404s for a week, fallback to gen 5 pixel art), avatars and the photo → sprite filter (corner flood-fill background removal, crop, premultiplied box downscale, light unsharp mask, 32-colour palette from the visible pixels, outline).
- Drawn sprites (`avatar_draw`, a job): up to four reference photos and up to three style pictures (`avatar_style_get`: the user's pictures in `data/style_refs`, then the trainer sprites of `prospero_style_sprites`, enlarged without smoothing on white and put side by side on one sheet; given apart, the model drew the reference line-up or copied one outfit) are imported into a "Silver's Hoard" project of Prospero's Hoard through the hub (`studio_import`, every write with a `reason`; style pictures once per content, their asset ids kept in `data/style_assets.json`). `studio_generate_image` gets them as `reference_asset_ids`, photos first, and a prompt that names them by number ("images 1 to 4" are the person, "image 5 is only a style reference", "exactly one character") plus the style text of the settings (aspect 2:3; `prospero_engine` picks the engine, else Prospero's default, Qwen-Image 2.1). `studio_job` is polled (six missed answers in a row before giving up); the Prospero job id is kept on the Silver job, so a restart cancels the render it leaves behind. Each picture is cut out with `studio_edit_image remove_background` (BiRefNet; the local edge fill when it is missing), read from Prospero by asset id (`/api/assets/{id}/file`: tool results through the hub are capped at 20 KB) and kept twice: the drawing, and the 96-px sprite (box shrink, light unsharp mask, palette from the visible pixels only).
- Badge pictures: `gyms.badge_image` is `shape:<id>` (18 shapes drawn by `client/src/components/badges.jsx`, the same file in the playable page) or `file:<name>` (an upload through `/api/badges/upload` or `badge_image_set`, or a `badge_draw` job: Prospero draws badge pins from a description, cut out and kept up to 256 px). Exports carry `badge_image: {kind: shape|image}`; earned badges copy the picture.
- Share codes carry the trainer's own picture too, shrunk to 48 KB at most (`CODE_PICTURE_BYTES`).
- `facilities.py` — the battle facilities (generation, singles or doubles, Pokémon per side, bosses with their battle number and character variant, the Battle Tree's specials), the era's trainer classes and names. A streak is a run of kind `facility` whose stages grow one battle at a time: `_facility_stage` seeds a `random.Random` with run id and battle number and returns the boss's team (a sample of its pool when it has more), now and then a special rival, or a generated trainer with Showdown random sets of the facility's generation (doubles sets from gen 8); rivals are normal for battles 1-7, hard for 8-14, expert after. `_facility_rules`: the challenger's format (a doubles custom game of its generation for a doubles facility), `Picked Team Size` (with `Team Preview` when the format lacks it) and level 50. A win adds a stage instead of ending the run; `auto` chains until a loss or `stop_at`; one open streak per trainer and facility. Records come from the runs (`facilities_list`).
- `agent_tools.py` — the 63 tools; `api/` — routes; `main.py` — the app factory (guard, PWA, SPA, copilot from Hoard Link).

## Client (`client/src`)

- `battle/` — shared by the app and the playable page: `protocol.js` (protocol reducer + sentences in Spanish/English), `player.js` (timed playback, seek, skip), `Arena.jsx` (field, sprites with Web Animations, HP cards, field effects, trainer bubbles, log), `Controls.jsx` (team preview, moves, targets, gimmicks, switches, forced switches, replay bar), `dex.js` (types, sprite ids, Spanish names, sprite URLs).
- `pages/` — Inicio, Combatir (battle / series / tournament), Combate, Trabajo (series stats, bracket, table), Entrenadores, Entrenador (team cards, team builder, text, AI, lines, battles), Gimnasios / Gimnasio (editor with the badge picture picker, challenge, badge holders), Ligas / Liga, Personajes (games as cards with sections by role, or by character) / Personaje (sprites, teams by game, own team), Historial, Medallas, Compartir, Ajustes.
- `components/TeamBuilder.jsx` — the visual team builder: move slots that open the list of learnable moves (type, category, power, accuracy, PP, Spanish in-game descriptions from `battle/descs-es.js` loaded on demand, filters, typed moves still accepted) and stat points (`engine/src/points.mjs`) or classic EVs/IVs; `components/badges.jsx` — badge shapes and pictures.
- `play/` — the playable page: target card, the friend's team (paste, random, trainer file), validation with the gym's rules, the stages one after another, result code and result file.

## Real time (`engine/src/realtime`, `client/src/battle/rt`, `silvers_hoard/pmd.py`)

- `za-data.mjs` — Legends: Z-A's base cooldowns of its 337 moves (Serebii, Bulbapedia), its power changes for fast and multi-hit moves, hits per use, and the Speed rule's constants.
- `moves.mjs` — `rtMove(id, gen)`: a move as real time uses it: base cooldown (Z-A's, else `derivedCooldown` from PP, power and kind, mean error 1.3 s against Z-A's table), `finalCooldown` (3 s off per 100 Speed over 25, floor 3 s, priority 3 s), wind-up ticks, shape (`strike`, `dash`, `shot`, `beam`, `wave`, `cone`, `burst`, `drop`, `self`, `field`), reach and the effects the simulator applies. `cooldownTable` for screens and tools.
- `team.mjs` — format id, generation and sets for real time without core.mjs's random-team generator (keeps the browser chunk smaller).
- `sim.mjs` — `RtBattle`: 30 ticks per second, a 28 × 17 m arena, one Pokémon per side. Each tick every seat gives an input `{mx, my, use, dodge, sw}` (people from the screen, AI seats from `brain.mjs`); acts are `approach` (walk into range), `windup`, `dash`, `roll` (invulnerable), `recover` (after recharge moves) and `switch`. Damage is the turn-based formula with abilities and items; one-stage timed boosts, timed statuses, residual effects every 4 s, weather, terrain, screens, hazards, protection, substitutes, pivots and forced switches. `view()` is what the screen draws; `summary()` has the classic summary's shape plus `mode`, `seconds` and `reason`; `eventLines` writes the stored English log. `rtRun(spec, {inputs, forfeit, tick})` and `fastForward` replay. Determinism: seeded generators (`makeRng`), fixed ticks, `Math.sqrt` instead of `Math.hypot`, no trigonometry; Node and Chrome give identical summaries.
- `brain.mjs` — the AI's reflexes: reaction time, dodge chance and switching by level; move scores (expected damage, setup, status, protection, recovery, hazards) weighted by style; spacing for melee or ranged moves; picking the next Pokémon by matchup.
- Engine commands: `rt_check` (validate a spec, first view), `rt_team` (a team's real stats and cooldowns), `rt_cooldowns`, `rt_play_out` (AI vs AI), `rt_replay` (inputs, or a forfeit at a tick). `stats.mjs` plays series and tournaments in either mode.
- App: battles carry `mode` and `replay` ({time_limit, inputs, tick}); a live real-time battle saves its inputs every 5 s (`battle_rt_save`) and ends with `battle_rt_finish`, which replays them in the engine and stores that result; watched ones run `rt_play_out` as a background job while the screen replays the same battle. Runs (gym and league challenges) keep their mode for every stage.
- `pmd.py` — downloads SpriteCollab's `AnimData.xml` and sheets of a Pokémon (forms through the tracker, shiny under `0000/0001`), works out each animation's ground anchor from its `-Shadow.png`, caches meta and sheets; routes `/api/sprites/pmd/{dex}` and `/api/sprites/pmd-sheet/{key}/{anim}`; starting a real-time battle prefetches both teams.
- Client: `RtScreen.jsx` (loop, keyboard and gamepad through `input.js` with the Z-A layout by default (Shift / ZL lock-on drawn as a reticle, moves in an X A B Y diamond, Space / Y roll, Q / E / R and the D-pad for the party, + pause) or the simple one, HUD, pause and forfeit, result, replay with speeds), `render.js` (canvas: court, trainers, depth-sorted sprites with `pmd.js`'s animator, telegraphs, floating numbers, screen shake), `vfx.js` (the look of each move, drawing only: rays from the user to the beam's head by move, shaped projectiles and trails, melee marks by move name, drops from the sky, bursts and quakes by move, hit particles by type, boost and heal auras, status particles, weather), `ModeFields.jsx` (the mode switch in battles, challenges, series and tournaments), `CooldownsTab.jsx` (the trainer page's real-time tab). Words in `rt/strings.js`, merged into the app's dictionary.

## Data formats

- `.hoardtrainer` `{kind: "silvers-hoard/trainer", schema: 1, app, owner, exported_at, trainer: {name, title, owner, format, team, ai, dialogue, notes, avatar: {kind: showdown|image}, source_id}}`; `.hoardgym` has `gym: {…, badge_image: {kind: shape, shape} | {kind: image, mime, data}, leader, trainers}`; `.hoardleague` `league: {…, gyms, elite_four, champion}`.
- Result codes: `{kind, gym_id | trainer_id, gym_name | trainer_name, challenger, won, turns, date, team, team_paste, stages, log?}`.

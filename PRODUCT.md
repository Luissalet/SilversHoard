## Platform

web

## Product purpose

Silver's Hoard lets Luis create and manage trainers, teams, gyms and leagues, play or watch Pokémon battles, and share playable pages. It includes classic and real-time combat, mass battles, facilities, leisure activities, history, music and analysis tools.

## Capabilities and constraints

Luis confirmed on 22 October 2026 that this visual work changes menus, interface effects and combat animation. Gameplay, navigation destinations and all existing functions must remain available. Team data, saved battles and replay rules must be preserved. This scope does not require changing the simulation or adding game modes.

## Brand commitments and evidence

Keep the Silver's Hoard name and existing emblem. Luis supplied a Pocket Brawl video, BigKidNation and Pokémon Battlegrounds references, and local sprites. Existing supplied-sprite metadata retains original art provenance. UI remains available in Spanish and English; the existing saved language preference is preserved.

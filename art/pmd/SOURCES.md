# Artwork and movement references

Pikachu-Gmax uses Gemini web-generated right profiles, four explicitly mirrored left poses and eight manually corrected front/back poses, following the user's request to edit pixels with code. The native sheets contain twelve authored figures and four mirrors, not sixteen independent drawings. CHUNSOFT's base Pikachu movement reference remains attributed to the pinned SpriteCollab revision and its CC BY-NC 4.0 terms; it is not a published Gigantamax sheet. Canonical HOME normal/shiny references and hashes remain in the native metadata. The shiny preserves the visible front/back tail and cloud RGB while recolouring the body. The registry records exact source hashes, authoring recipe hashes and the independent anatomy, palette and movement review.

Published sources retain their own artists and terms. A repository maintainer is not assumed to be an individual drawing author. The registry distinguishes generated sheets, user-supplied walking atlases and published indexed overworld art.

The original Mega Swampert sheet is by [Jhony-Rex](https://www.deviantart.com/jhony-rex/art/Mega-Swampert-sprites-499321596). The original Delphox-line walking sheet, including Mega Delphox, is by [CamiLovesO](https://www.deviantart.com/camiloveso/art/1276413751). These supplied pixels take priority over generated replacements.

Generated Mega Chesnaught and Mega Baxcalibur profile repairs used authored PMD walking sequences as choreography references. Those reference drawings are from [SpriteCollab revision341342d7ef2ed3e4427c760e829a417b501be17c](https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/tree/341342d7ef2ed3e4427c760e829a417b501be17c), under [CC BY-NC4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): Chesnaught by Soulja; Baxcalibur by Soulja and pi3.14. Scolipede reference poses are by baronessfaron; Braviary reference poses are by Caitemis. Referenced published pixels are not relabelled as generated originals. The selected generated sheets record their authoring and repair prompts separately.

Canonical anatomy was reinforced with official artwork: [Mega Chesnaught](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/story-world/pokemon/mega-chesnaught), [Mega Baxcalibur](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/dlc), [Mega Scolipede](https://zukan.pokemon.co.jp/detail/0545-1), [Mega Glimmora](https://www.pokemonkorea.co.kr/pokedex/view/1229), and [Galarian Darmanitan](https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-us/pokemon-galar-region/darmanitan/). Anatomical references are not evidence of an official walking cycle.

Published HGSS-style overworld art and its collective credits/terms are retained under `published/hg-engine/`. Those sources have two authored locomotion phases in four headings; the adapter does not invent an Attack sheet or describe them as sixteen PMD poses.

Additional published walking art comes from [Soulgold revision4d4acbe987c5aaf3e5580ccf48c3f0c68beebde5](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/tree/4d4acbe987c5aaf3e5580ccf48c3f0c68beebde5). Its README and collective credits travel under `published/soulgold/`; no per-file drawing author or new permissive licence is inferred. Three authored headings and the explicitly mirrored right heading retain their actual two movement phases, without an invented Attack.

The Terapagos Stellar rear repair references Terapagos Terastal anatomy by G〜 and JFain from the same pinned SpriteCollab revision. Separately authored shiny sheets retain their own source hashes and visual reviews. A normal sheet's crop-preservation receipts are historical parent information, not a claim that the shiny redraw has identical geometry.

Falinks uses the published Brass and Trooper components from that same SpriteCollab revision: Brass normal/shiny by ◥θ┴θ◤, Trooper normal by baronessfaron, and Trooper shiny by Emmuffin, under CC BY-NC 4.0. One Brass and five Troopers are arranged with fixed offsets without resampling their original pixels. The full sheets retain eight directions and four Walk frames with the native 6/8/6/8 tick clock, plus Attack, Charge, Hurt, Faint and Sleep; Idle uses the first published pose. Per-component credits and hashes remain in each variant's metadata. This composition replaces the generated Falinks formation.

Base Meganium (0154), Eevee (0133) and Venusaur (0003) walking sequences, credited to CHUNSOFT in the pinned SpriteCollab tracker, also informed the generated locomotion repairs. These are base-species movement references, not published Mega/Gigantamax sheets. The selected Eevee profile phase was authored through Gemini's web image mode, using its automatic Flash Alto response when Pro was unavailable. Its record identifies a display-resolution browser capture and the technical crop/background removal; it is not described as a full-resolution download. The other profile phase and retained poses keep their separate original provenance.

The four Ogerpon Tera shiny palettes were checked against the exact giant-mask Tera front sprites and indexed palettes in the pinned Soulgold repository. Those are published fan references, not official HOME renders. Their raw files, decoded palettes and source hashes are recorded separately from the generated poses; the repository's collective credits remain intact. Other separately authored shiny corrections were checked against the corresponding aligned normal/shiny HOME references, with URLs and hashes retained in their generation metadata.

Pecharunt's published normal and shiny Idle sheets at the pinned revision are byte-identical (SHA-256 `203fd741f2ada7b314bba6a73598b6a04262a4e2bd10dce558fbb5a14ad13bc3`); the collected eight animation sheets also match. Its shiny correction uses aligned [normal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/sprites/pokemon/other/home/1025.png) and [shiny HOME](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/sprites/pokemon/other/home/shiny/1025.png) palette references. G〜 retains the original PMD drawing credit and CC BY-NC 4.0 terms. The golden inner body, magenta shell and appendages were reviewed separately; alpha, frames and timing retain their native values. Some RGB-black pixels become near-black blue during palette transfer; exact outline RGB equality is not claimed.

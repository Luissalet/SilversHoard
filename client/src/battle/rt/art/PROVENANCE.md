# Original real-time arena floors

Generated on 16 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool for Luis's Silver polish request. These are original environment illustrations; no creature art, sprites, logos or third-party textures were used as input. The original generated PNGs were encoded to WebP (ImageMagick, quality 88) and embedded as data URLs in arena-art.js, so the app and exported playable pages use identical self-contained images. Only the floor appearance changes; collision geometry, battle rules and stored results remain unchanged.

Champion: 409358 bytes WebP. Summit: 284718 bytes WebP. The procedural floor remains available while an image loads or if decoding fails. Court lines, rocks, trainers, audiences and atmospheric effects are drawn separately. Surface wear in the images is cosmetic and never implies additional obstacles.

## Champion prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game floor texture for an overhead 2D creature-battle arena, landscape 28:17 ratio. Primary request: a richly detailed champion hall floor, orthographic straight down, entire image is walkable flat floor. Muted deep teal cut stone tiles, subtle worn jade marble grain, fine brass inlaid joints, elegant perimeter mosaic in muted antique gold, small warm amber mineral flecks and meticulous hand-painted cracks. Art direction: polished high-detail pixel-art JRPG environment, crisp clustered pixels and controlled texture, nuanced material shading, cool emerald palette with aged gold accents. Composition: rectangular floor texture fills frame edge-to-edge; outer 8 percent has intricate decorative border, central 75 percent remains quiet and even in value so tiny sprites and red/orange warning circles read clearly. Tile scale roughly 2m per tile on 28m by17m floor. Keep all tiles exactly flat and coplanar. Lighting: even diffuse overhead illumination, gentle ambient variation, no harsh spotlight. Constraints: top down orthographic, no perspective, no walls, no columns, no raised steps, no rocks, no holes, no furniture, no creatures, no characters, no court lines or ball emblem (engine overlays these), no text, no UI, no watermark. This is an actual terrain asset, not a screenshot or presentation.
